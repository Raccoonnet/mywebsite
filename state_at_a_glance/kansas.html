Kansas at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: KS
  • Capital: Topeka
  • Largest City: Wichita
  • Region: Midwest
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT) & Mountain Tim (MT)
  • Founded: 1861
  • State Number: 34
  • Population: ~3.0 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 82,300 mi²
  • Population Density: 36.45 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Sunflower State, The Wheat State, America's Heartland
  • Climate: Continental with four distinct seasons, warm summers, cool winters, and frequent spring thunderstorms
  • Major Industries: Agriculture, aviation, manufacturing, energy, healthcare, education, logistics
  • Landmarks: Flint Hills, Monument Rocks, Tallgrass Prairie National Preserve, Eisenhower Presidential Library, Strataca Underground Salt Museum
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Wichita Dwight D. Eisenhower National Airport (ICT)
    • Interstates North-South: I-35, I-35W
    • Interstates East-West: I-66, I-70
  • Cultural Highlights: County fairs, Native American heritage, cowboy traditions, jazz, local museums, and community festivals
  • Known For: Sunflowers
