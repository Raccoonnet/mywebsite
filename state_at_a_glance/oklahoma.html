Oklahoma at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: OK
  • Capital: Oklahoma City
  • Largest City: Oklahoma City
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1907
  • State Number: 46
  • Population: ~4.1 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 69,699 mi²
  • Population Density: 58.82 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Native America, Land of the Red Man, The Sooner State
  • Climate: Humid subtropical with seasonal extremes
  • Major Industries: Energy, agriculture, aviation, manufacturing
  • Landmarks: Tallgrass Prairie Preserve, Wichita Mountains, Route 66
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Will Rogers World Airport (OKC)
    • Interstates North-South: I-35
    • Interstates East-West: I-40, I-44
  • Cultural Highlights: Native American heritage, country & red dirt music, rodeos, local festivals
  • Known For: Native American heritage
