Minnesota at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: MN
  • Capital: Saint Paul
  • Largest City: Minneapolis
  • Region: Midwest
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1858
  • State Number: 32
  • Population: ~5.8 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 86,936 mi²
  • Population Density: 66.72 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): North Star State, Gopher State, Land of 10,000 Lakes, Land of the Sky Blue Waters
  • Climate: Humid continental with warm summers, cold snowy winters, and colorful spring and autumn seasons
  • Major Industries: Healthcare, manufacturing, agriculture, technology, finance, retail, mining, and food production
  • Landmarks: Boundary Waters Canoe Area Wilderness, Lake Superior North Shore, Mississippi River Headwaters, Voyageurs National Park, Mall of America, Split Rock Lighthouse
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Minneapolis-Saint Paul International Airport (MSP)
    • Interstates North-South: I-35, I-35E, I-35W
    • Interstates East-West: I-90, I-94
  • Cultural Highlights: Scandinavian heritage, Indigenous cultures, thriving arts scene, music, lakeside recreation, and year-round festivals
  • Known For: Lakes
