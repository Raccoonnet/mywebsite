Wisconsin at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: WI
  • Capital: Madison
  • Largest City: Milwaukee
  • Region: Midwest
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1848
  • State Number: 30
  • Population: ~5.9 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 65,498 mi²
  • Population Density: 90.08 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Badger State, America's Dairyland
  • Climate: Humid continental; cold winters with snow, warm summers
  • Major Industries: Agriculture, manufacturing, healthcare, IT, tourism
  • Landmarks: Milwaukee Art Museum, Apostle Islands, Lambeau Field
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: General Mitchell International Airport (MKE)
    • Interstates North-South: I-39, I-41, I-43
    • Interstates East-West: I-90, I-94
  • Cultural Highlights: Summerfest, State Fair, breweries, Native American heritage
  • Known For: Dairy products
