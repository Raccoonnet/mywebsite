Iowa at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: IA
  • Capital: Des Moines
  • Largest City: Des Moines
  • Region: Midwest
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1846
  • State Number: 29
  • Population: ~3.2 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 56,273 mi²
  • Population Density: 56.87 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Hawkeye State
  • Climate: Humid continental; snowy winters, warm summers, seasonal storms
  • Major Industries: Agriculture, insurance, manufacturing, wind energy
  • Landmarks: Effigy Mounds, Loess Hills, Bridges of Madison County
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Des Moines International Airport (DSM)
    • Interstates North-South: I-29, I-35
    • Interstates East-West: I-74, I-80
  • Cultural Highlights: Iowa State Fair, Writers’ Workshop, county fairs, quilt trails
  • Known For: Farming
