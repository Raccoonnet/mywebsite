Louisiana at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: LA
  • Capital: Baton Rouge
  • Largest City: New Orleans
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1812
  • State Number: 18
  • Population: ~4.6 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 52,271 mi²
  • Population Density: 88.00 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Pelican State, Bayou State, Creole State, Sportsman's Paradise, The Boot
  • Climate: Humid subtropical with hot summers and mild winters
  • Major Industries: Petrochemicals, oil and gas, tourism, agriculture, shipping, music and entertainment
  • Landmarks: French Quarter, Mardi Gras, Cajun Bayou, Lake Pontchartrain, Creole plantations
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY)
    • Interstates North-South: I-49, I-55, I-59, I-69
    • Interstates East-West: I-10, I-12, I-14, I-20
  • Cultural Highlights: Jazz, Cajun and Creole cuisine, Mardi Gras, Mardi Gras Indians, historic architecture
  • Known For: Cuisine
