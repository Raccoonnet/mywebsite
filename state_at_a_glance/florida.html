Florida at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: FL
  • Capital: Tallahassee
  • Largest City: Jacksonville
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET) & Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1845
  • State Number: 27
  • Population: ~22.5 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 65,758 mi²
  • Population Density: 342.16 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Sunshine State
  • Climate: Subtropical to tropical; hot, humid summers, mild winters; hurricane season June–Nov
  • Major Industries: Tourism, aerospace, agriculture, finance, trade, real estate
  • Landmarks: Everglades, Keys, Kennedy Space Center, Biscayne Bay, Dry Tortugas
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Miami International Airport (MIA)
    • Interstates North-South: I-75, I-75E, I-95
    • Interstates East-West: I-4, I-10
  • Cultural Highlights: Cuban heritage, Seminole culture, Wynwood arts, music festivals
  • Known For: Beaches
