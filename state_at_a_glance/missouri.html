Missouri at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: MO
  • Capital: Jefferson City
  • Largest City: Kansas City
  • Region: Midwest
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1821
  • State Number: 24
  • Population: ~6.1 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 69,715 mi²
  • Population Density: 87.50 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Show-Me State, Cave State, Mother of the West
  • Climate: Humid continental; hot summers, cold winters, storms
  • Major Industries: Aerospace, transport, agriculture, brewing, manufacturing
  • Landmarks: Gateway Arch, Ozarks, Mark Twain Forest, Lake of the Ozarks
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: St. Louis Lambert International Airport (STL)
    • Interstates North-South: I-29, I-35, I-49, I-55, I-57
    • Interstates East-West: I-44, I-64, I-66, I-70, I-72
  • Cultural Highlights: Jazz, BBQ, Shakespeare Festival, Route 66 sites
  • Known For: Farming
