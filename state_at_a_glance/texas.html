Texas at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: TX
  • Capital: Austin
  • Largest City: Houston
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT) & Mountain Time (MT)
  • Founded: 1845
  • State Number: 28
  • Population: ~30 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 268,596 mi²
  • Population Density: 111.69 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Lone Star State
  • Climate: Varies widely — arid west, humid east, hot summers, mild winters
  • Major Industries: Energy, tech, agriculture, aerospace, manufacturing, healthcare
  • Landmarks: The Alamo, Big Bend NP, NASA Johnson Space Center, TX State Capitol
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (DFW)
    • Interstates North-South: I-27, I-35, I-35E, I-35W, I-37, I-45, I-49, I-69, I-69C, I-69E, I-69W
    • Interstates East-West: I-2, I-10, I-14, I-20, I-30, I-40, I-44
  • Cultural Highlights: SXSW, Houston Rodeo, TX State Fair, cowboy heritage
  • Known For: Cowboy heritage
