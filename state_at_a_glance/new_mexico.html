New Mexico at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: NM
  • Capital: Santa Fe
  • Largest City: Albuquerque
  • Region: West
  • Time Zone: Mountain Time (MT)
  • Founded: 1912
  • State Number: 47
  • Population: ~2.1 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 121,589 mi²
  • Population Density: 17.27 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Land of Enchantment
  • Climate: Generally dry and sunny, but highly varied because of elevation; mountains experience cold winters while lower elevations have hot summers and cool nights.
  • Major Industries: Energy, federal research and national laboratories, agriculture, tourism, film, technology, manufacturing, and outdoor recreation.
  • Landmarks: Rio Grande, White Sands, Carlsbad Caverns, Chaco Canyon, Bandelier National Monument, Taos Pueblo, Sangre de Cristo Mountains, and the state's extensive high-desert landscapes.
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Albuquerque International Sunport (ABQ)
    • Interstates North-South: I-25
    • Interstates East-West: I-10, I-40
  • Cultural Highlights: Pueblo communities, Native American and Hispanic arts, Santa Fe's galleries, Albuquerque's festivals and flamenco traditions, traditional markets, museums, and chile-centered foodways.
  • Known For: Green Chile
