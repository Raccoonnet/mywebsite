Maryland at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: MD
  • Capital: Annapolis
  • Largest City: Baltimore
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1788
  • State Number: 7
  • Population: ~6.3 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 12,407 mi²
  • Population Density: 507.78 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Old Line State, Free State, Little America, America in Miniature
  • Climate: Humid subtropical in central and eastern regions; cooler and more mountainous in the west
  • Major Industries: Government and defense, biotechnology, maritime industries, tourism, and healthcare
  • Landmarks: Chesapeake Bay, Fort McHenry, Antietam National Battlefield
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Baltimore/Washington International Thurgood Marshall Airport (BWI)
    • Interstates North-South: I-81, I-83, I-95, I-97
    • Interstates East-West: I-68, I-70
  • Cultural Highlights: Maritime heritage, colonial history, diverse neighborhoods, and a distinctive state flag recognized nationwide
  • Known For: Crab Cakes
