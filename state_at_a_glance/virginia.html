Virginia at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: VA
  • Capital: Richmond
  • Largest City: Virginia Beach
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1788
  • State Number: 10
  • Population: ~8.9 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 42,775 mi²
  • Population Density: 208.07 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Old Dominion, Mother of Presidents
  • Climate: Humid subtropical in the east; cooler and more mountainous in the west
  • Major Industries: Film and television, agriculture, manufacturing, tourism, aerospace, tech
  • Landmarks: Colonial Williamsburg, Shenandoah National Park, Arlington National Cemetery
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Washington Dulles International Airport (IAD)
    • Interstates North-South: I-77, I-81, I-85, I-95
    • Interstates East-West: I-64, I-66
  • Cultural Highlights: Colonial heritage, presidential homes, mountain music traditions, and coastal maritime life
  • Known For: U.S. History
