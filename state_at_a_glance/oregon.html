Oregon at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: OR
  • Capital: Salem
  • Largest City: Portland
  • Region: West
  • Time Zone: Pacific Time (PT)
  • Founded: 1859
  • State Number: 33
  • Population: ~4.3 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 98,381 mi²
  • Population Density: 43.71 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Beaver State
  • Climate: Varied; wet and mild along much of the west, cooler in the mountains, and considerably drier across Central and Eastern Oregon.
  • Major Industries: Agriculture, forestry, manufacturing, technology, food and beverage, outdoor products, clean energy, and services.
  • Landmarks: Mount Hood, Crater Lake, the Columbia River Gorge, Oregon Coast, high desert, Willamette Valley, and numerous state and national parks.
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Portland International Airport (PDX)
    • Interstates North-South: I-5
    • Interstates East-West: I-82, I-84
  • Cultural Highlights: Craft brewing, wine, independent music, bookstores, visual arts, farmers’ markets, outdoor recreation, and a strong local-food tradition.Craft brewing, wine, independent music, bookstores, visual arts, farmers’ markets, outdoor recreation, and a strong local-food tradition.
  • Known For: Evergreen forests
