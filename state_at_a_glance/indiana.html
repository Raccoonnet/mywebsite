Indiana at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: IN
  • Capital: Indianapolis
  • Largest City: Indianapolis
  • Region: Midwest
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (MT) & Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1816
  • State Number: 19
  • Population: ~6.8 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 36,420 mi²
  • Population Density: 186.71 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Hoosier State
  • Climate: Humid continental; cold winters, hot summers, thunderstorms
  • Major Industries: Auto manufacturing, agriculture, logistics, pharma
  • Landmarks: Indy Motor Speedway, Indiana Dunes, Amish Country
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Indianapolis International Airport (IND)
    • Interstates North-South: I-65, I-69
    • Interstates East-West: I-64, I-70, I-74, I-80, I-90, I-94
  • Cultural Highlights: Indy 500, quilt trails, covered bridges, Bloomington music
  • Known For: Indy 500
