Ohio at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: OH
  • Capital: Columbus
  • Largest City: Columbus
  • Region: Midwest
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1803
  • State Number: 17
  • Population: ~11.7 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 44,825 mi²
  • Population Density: 261.02 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Buckeye State, Birthplace of Aviation, The Heart of It All
  • Climate: Humid continental; cold winters, warm summers, lake-effect snow
  • Major Industries: Manufacturing, healthcare, finance, education, tech, agriculture
  • Landmarks: Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, Cuyahoga Valley NP, Pro Football Hall of Fame
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: John Glenn Columbus International Airport (CMH)
    • Interstates North-South: I-71, I-73, I-75, I-77
    • Interstates East-West: I-70, I-74, I-76, I-80, I-80N, I-80S, I-90
  • Cultural Highlights: Cincinnati Music Fest, Cleveland Orchestra, Ohio State Fair
  • Known For: Birthplace of aviation
