Nebraska at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: NE
  • Capital: Lincoln
  • Largest City: Omaha
  • Region: Midwest
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1867
  • State Number: 37
  • Population: ~1.9 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 77,358 mi²
  • Population Density: 24.56 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Cornhuster State
  • Climate: Continental; hot summers, cold winters, moderate precipitation
  • Major Industries: Agriculture, manufacturing, transportation, finance, IT
  • Landmarks: Chimney Rock, Scotts Bluff, Sandhills, Henry Doorly Zoo
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Eppley Airport (OMA)
    • Interstates North-South: NA
    • Interstates East-West: I-76, I-80, I-80S
  • Cultural Highlights: College World Series, State Fair, Native American events
  • Known For: Farming
