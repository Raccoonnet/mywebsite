Georgia at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: GA
  • Capital: Atlanta
  • Largest City: Atlanta
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1733
  • State Number: 4
  • Population: ~11.2 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 59,420 mi²
  • Population Density: 188.49 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Peach State, Empire State of the South
  • Climate: Humid subtropical with hot summers and mild winters
  • Major Industries: Film and television, agriculture, manufacturing, tourism, aerospace, tech
  • Landmarks: Savannah Historic District, Stone Mountain, Okefenokee Swamp, Georgia Aquarium, Atlanta Botanical Garden
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL)
    • Interstates North-South: I-59, I-75, I-85, I-95
    • Interstates East-West: I-3, I-14, I-16, I-20, I-24
  • Cultural Highlights: Southern literature, jazz and hip-hop music scenes, historic architecture, college football traditions
  • Known For: Peaches
