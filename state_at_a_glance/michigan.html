Michigan at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: MI
  • Capital: Lansing
  • Largest City: Detroit
  • Region: Midwest
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1837
  • State Number: 26
  • Population: ~10.1 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 96,716 mi²
  • Population Density: 104.43 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Great Lakes State, The Wolverine State, Water (Winter) Wonderland
  • Climate: Humid continental; cold winters, warm summers, lake-effect snow in many regions
  • Major Industries: Automotive manufacturing, agriculture, tourism, technology, manufacturing
  • Landmarks: Mackinac Bridge, Sleeping Bear Dunes, Isle Royale, Great Lakes shoreline
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport (DTW)
    • Interstates North-South: I-69, I-75
    • Interstates East-West: I-94, I-96
  • Cultural Highlights: Motown music, automotive heritage, lakefront living, strong sports culture
  • Known For: The Great Lakes
