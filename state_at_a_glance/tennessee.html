Tennessee at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: TN
  • Capital: Nashville
  • Largest City: Memphis
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT) & Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1796
  • State Number: 16
  • Population: ~6.9 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 42,143 mi²
  • Population Density: 163.73 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Volunteer State
  • Climate: Humid subtropical; hot summers, mild winters, occasional snow
  • Major Industries: Music, manufacturing, agriculture, healthcare, tourism
  • Landmarks: Great Smoky Mountains NP, Graceland, Country Music Hall of Fame
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Nashville International Airport (BNA)
    • Interstates North-South: I-3, I-55, I-65, I-69, I-75, I-81
    • Interstates East-West: I-24, I-26, I-40
  • Cultural Highlights: CMA Music Festival, Memphis Blues Festival, Civil War sites
  • Known For: Country music
