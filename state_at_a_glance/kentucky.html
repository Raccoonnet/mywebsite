Kentucky at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: KY
  • Capital: Frankfort
  • Largest City: Louisville
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET) & Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1792
  • State Number: 15
  • Population: ~4.5 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 40,408 mi²
  • Population Density: 111.36 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Bluegrass State
  • Climate: Humid subtropical; hot summers, mild winters, year-round rain
  • Major Industries: Bourbon, horses, auto manufacturing, coal, agriculture
  • Landmarks: Mammoth Cave, Derby, Bourbon Trail, Cumberland Falls
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport (SDF)
    • Interstates North-South: I-65, I-69, I-71, I-75
    • Interstates East-West: I-24, I-64, I-66
  • Cultural Highlights: Derby Festival, bluegrass music, craft fairs, horse farms
  • Known For: Bourbon
