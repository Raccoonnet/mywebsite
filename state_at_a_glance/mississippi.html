Mississippi at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: MS
  • Capital: Jackson
  • Largest City: Jackson
  • Region: South
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1817
  • State Number: 20
  • Population: ~2.9 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 48,434 mi²
  • Population Density: 59.88 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Magnolia State, The Hospitality State, The Sipp, Birthplace of America's Music
  • Climate: Humid subtropical with hot summers and mild winters
  • Major Industries: Agriculture (cotton, soybeans, poultry), manufacturing, shipbuilding, energy, tourism
  • Landmarks: Mississippi River, Natchez Trace Parkway, Vicksburg National Military Park, Gulf Coast beaches, blues heritage sites
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Jackson-Medgar Wiley Evers International Airport (JAN)
    • Interstates North-South: I-55, I-59, I-69
    • Interstates East-West: I-10, I-14, I-20, I-22
  • Cultural Highlights: Blues music heritage, Southern literature, Delta traditions, annual festivals, historic architecture
  • Known For: Blues music
