Washington at a Glance

  • Abbreviation: WA
  • Capital: Olympia
  • Largest City: Seattle
  • Region: West
  • Time Zone: Pacific Time (PT)
  • Founded: 1889
  • State Number: 42
  • Population: ~8.0 million (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 71,362 mi²
  • Population Density: 112.10 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Evergreen State
  • Climate: Western Washington is generally cool and wet, while areas east of the Cascades are substantially drier, with colder winters and warmer summers.
  • Major Industries: Technology, aerospace, agriculture, forestry, trade, manufacturing, maritime industries, and life sciences.
  • Landmarks: Mount Rainier, Olympic National Park, North Cascades National Park, Puget Sound, Columbia River, San Juan Islands, and the Cascade Range.
  • Transportation:
    • Primary Airport: Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA)
    • Interstates North-South: I-5
    • Interstates East-West: I-82, I-90
  • Cultural Highlights: Indigenous cultures, maritime traditions, Pacific Northwest music, visual arts, theater, coffee, craft brewing, wine, and a strong outdoor-recreation culture.
  • Known For: Natural beauty
