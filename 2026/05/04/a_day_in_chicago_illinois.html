A Day in Chicago, Illinois

04 May 2026

Chicago has a way of making even a single day feel full, vibrant, and memorable—and my recent visit was exactly that.

Breakfast

I kicked things off with a cozy breakfast at Goddess and the Baker, a charming spot just outside The Loop. It’s the kind of place that instantly sets the tone for the day: great coffee, fresh pastries, and a relaxed atmosphere that makes you want to linger just a little longer before heading out into the city.

Millennium Park

From there, I dove straight into some of Chicago’s most iconic sights. Millennium Park was buzzing with energy, and seeing “The Bean” in person absolutely lived up to the hype. Its reflective surface captures the skyline in a way that feels almost surreal. Just steps away, I wandered past the Jay Pritzker Pavilion, through the peaceful paths of Lurie Garden, and over to Buckingham Fountain, each stop offering a different slice of the city’s personality—modern, artistic, and beautifully designed.

Of course, no Chicago day is complete without a snack break, so I made my way to Garrett’s Popcorn. Their famous Chicago-style mix—cheddar and caramel—is one of those combinations that sounds unusual but works perfectly. Sweet, savory, and a little addictive.

The Mile

In the afternoon, I hopped on public transit and headed over to the Magnificent Mile. This stretch is a shopper’s paradise, but even just walking it is an experience. The highlight for me was the Starbucks Reserve Roastery—five floors dedicated to coffee in every form imaginable. It’s part café, part spectacle, and completely worth the visit whether you’re a coffee enthusiast or not.

Navy Pier

To wrap up the day, I made my way to Navy Pier just as the evening settled in. The lakefront views and lively atmosphere made for a perfect ending. And, of course, I couldn’t leave without trying Chicago’s legendary deep-dish pizza. A stop at Giordano’s delivered exactly what I was hoping for: rich, cheesy, and so thick you really do need a fork and knife.

If you’re planning a day in Chicago, this kind of mix—great food, iconic landmarks, and a bit of wandering—makes for an unforgettable experience. It’s a city that rewards curiosity, and even in just one day, it gives you plenty to fall in love with.

Thanks for joining me on my quest to visit all 50 states!

Every mile brings a new story, a new discovery, and a new reason to love America.
