Omaha at a Glance

  • State: Nebraska
  • County: Douglas
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1854
  • Population: ~489,051 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 146.3 mi²
  • Population Density: 3,343.48 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Gate-city of the West, The Big O
  • Climate: Humid continental with hot summers and cold, snowy winters
  • Landmarks: Old Market, Henry Doorly Zoo & Aquarium, Bob Kerrey Pedestrian Bridge, Lauritzen Gardens
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Eppley Airfield (OMA)
    • Transit: Metro Transit
    • Highways: I‑29, US 75, I‑80, US 6
  • Cultural Highlights: Live music scene, college sports, community festivals, growing arts districts
  • Known For: Stockyards
