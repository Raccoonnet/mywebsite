Joplin at a Glance

  • State: Missouri
  • County: Jasper
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1873
  • Population: ~51,762 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 38.2 mi²
  • Population Density: 1,354.67 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): JoMo, The J-Town, Go Town USA
  • Climate: Humid subtropical—hot summers, cool winters, and vibrant spring and fall seasons
  • Landmarks: Grand Falls, Cunningham Park, Joplin History & Mineral Museum, historic U.S. Route 66 alignment
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Joplin Regional Airport (JLN)
    • Transit: Sunshine Lamp Trolly
    • Highways: US 71, I‑44, US 66
  • Cultural Highlights: Route 66 heritage, public art installations, community festivals, and a strong live-music tradition
  • Known For: Tornado resilience
