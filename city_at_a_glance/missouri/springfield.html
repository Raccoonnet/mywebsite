Springfield at a Glance

  • State: Missouri
  • County: Greene
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1834
  • Population: ~169,176 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 83.7 mi²
  • Population Density: 2,021.22 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Queen City of the Ozarks, Birthplace of Route 66
  • Climate: Humid subtropical—hot summers, mild to cool winters, and vibrant spring and fall seasons
  • Landmarks: Fantastic Caverns, Wonders of Wildlife National Museum & Aquarium, Missouri State University, Route 66 Car Museum
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Springfield–Branson National Airport (SGF)
    • Transit: City Utilities (CU)
    • Highways: US 65, US 13, I-44, US 60, US 160, US 66
  • Cultural Highlights: Route 66 heritage, university arts scene, live music, and Ozark outdoor recreation
  • Known For: Route 66
