St. Louis at a Glance

  • State: Missouri
  • County: St. Louis
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1764
  • Population: ~301,578 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 66.2 mi²
  • Population Density: 4,557.62 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Gateway to the West, The Gateway City, Mound City, The Lou, Rome of the West, River City, The STL, St. Lou
  • Climate: Humid subtropical—hot, humid summers and cool winters with occasional snow
  • Landmarks: Gateway Arch, Forest Park, St. Louis Cardinals, Saint Louis Zoo, Washington University in St. Louis
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: St. Louis Lambert International Airport (STL)
    • Transit: Metro Transit
    • Highways: I‑55, I‑270, US 61, US 67, I‑44, I‑64, I‑70, US 50
  • Cultural Highlights: Blues heritage, world-class (and often free) museums, sports loyalty, and distinctive neighborhood identities
  • Known For: Arch
