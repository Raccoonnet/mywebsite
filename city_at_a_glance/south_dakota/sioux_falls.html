Sioux Falls at a Glance

  • State: South Dakota
  • County: Minnehaha
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1879
  • Population: ~192,517 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 81.2 mi²
  • Population Density: 2,371.19 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Best Little City in America, Queen City of the West, The Heart of America
  • Climate: Four distinct seasons, with cold, snowy winters and warm, sometimes humid summers
  • Landmarks: Falls Park, the Big Sioux River Greenway, SculptureWalk, the Pettigrew Home & Museum, and the historic downtown district
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Sioux Falls Regional Airport (FSD)
    • Transit: Sioux Area Metro (SAM)
    • Highways: I-29, I-229, I-90
  • Cultural Highlights: Public sculpture, live music, independent restaurants and cafés, museums, community festivals, and a strong local arts presence
  • Known For: Falls Park
