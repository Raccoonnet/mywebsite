Oak Harbor at a Glance

  • State: Washington
  • County: Island
  • Time Zone: Pacific Time (PT)
  • Founded: 1915
  • Population: ~24,622 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 12.3 mi²
  • Population Density: 1,998.54 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): NA
  • Climate: Mild maritime climate, moderated by surrounding water; Whidbey Island's rain-shadow position contributes to relatively lower rainfall than Seattle.
  • Landmarks: Historic downtown, Windjammer Park, Oak Harbor Marina, Waterfront Trail, Pacific Northwest Naval Air Museum and nearby Deception Pass State Park.
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Seattle Paine Field International Airport (PAE)
    • Transit: Island Transit
    • Highways: WA-20
  • Cultural Highlights: Holland Happening, Oak Harbor Music Festival, military heritage, waterfront events, local theater, arts organizations and community celebrations.
  • Known For: Whidbey Island
