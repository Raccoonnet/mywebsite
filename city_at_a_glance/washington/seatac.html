SeaTac at a Glance

  • State: Washington
  • County: King
  • Time Zone: Pacific Time (PT)
  • Founded: 1990
  • Population: ~31,454 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 10.2 mi²
  • Population Density: 3,071.98 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): NA
  • Climate: Mild maritime Pacific Northwest climate, with cool, wet winters and generally mild summers.
  • Landmarks: Seattle-Tacoma International Airport, Angle Lake, Angle Lake Park, North SeaTac Park, Highline SeaTac Botanical Garden and the city's International Boulevard corridor.
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA)
    • Transit: King County Metro
    • Highways: WA-99, WA-509, WA-518
  • Cultural Highlights: International dining, community festivals, gardens, public parks, local history and a remarkably multilingual population. Spanish, Amharic/Somali languages, Vietnamese, Tagalog and Khmer are among the most commonly spoken non-English language groups identified by the city.
  • Known For: Airport
