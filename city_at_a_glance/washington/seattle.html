Seattle at a Glance

  • State: Washington
  • County: King
  • Time Zone: Pacific Time (PT)
  • Founded: 1851
  • Population: ~737,015 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 142.1 mi²
  • Population Density: 5,187.69 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Emerald City, Jet City, Rain City
  • Climate: Mild maritime climate, with cool, wet winters and generally mild, comparatively dry summers.
  • Landmarks: Pike Place Market, Space Needle, Seattle Center, Pioneer Square, Museum of Pop Culture, Chihuly Garden and Glass, Seattle Art Museum, Discovery Park and the waterfront.
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA)
    • Transit: King County Metro
    • Highways: I-5, WA-99, WA-509, I-90, WA-520, WA-522
  • Cultural Highlights: Live music, theater, museums, public art, neighborhood festivals, independent bookstores, international food traditions and a long history of innovation.
  • Known For: Starbucks
