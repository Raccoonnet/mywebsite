Mount Vernon at a Glance

  • State: Washington
  • County: Skagit
  • Time Zone: Pacific Time (PT)
  • Founded: 1889
  • Population: ~35,219 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 12.6 mi²
  • Population Density: 2,788.52 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): NA
  • Climate: Marine-influenced Pacific Northwest climate, with cool, wet winters and generally mild summers.
  • Landmarks: Skagit River, historic downtown, Skagit Valley farmland, nearby Little Mountain, and access to the North Cascades and Puget Sound region.
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Bellingham International Airport (BLI)
    • Transit: Skagit Transit
    • Highways: I-5, WA-538
  • Cultural Highlights: Downtown arts and events, local galleries, agricultural festivals, community markets, performing arts, and the wider Skagit Valley's museums and creative organizations.
  • Known For: Tulips
