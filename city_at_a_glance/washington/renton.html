Renton at a Glance

  • State: Washington
  • County: King
  • Time Zone: Pacific Time (PT)
  • Founded: 1885
  • Population: ~106,785 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 25.3 mi²
  • Population Density: 4,225.76 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): NA
  • Climate: Mild maritime Pacific Northwest climate, with cool, wet winters and generally mild summers.
  • Landmarks: Gene Coulon Memorial Beach Park, Lake Washington, Cedar River, Renton History Museum, historic downtown, Renton Civic Theatre and the Jimi Hendrix memorial.
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA)
    • Transit: King County Metro
    • Highways: I-405, WA-167, WA-169, WA-515, WA-900
  • Cultural Highlights: Renton River Days, public art, theater, music, multicultural events, farmers markets and the city's growing downtown arts programming.
  • Known For: Boeing
