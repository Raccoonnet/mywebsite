New Orleans at a Glance

  • State: Louisiana
  • County: Orleans
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1718
  • Population: ~383,997 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 349.9 mi²
  • Population Density: 1,097.60 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Crescent City, The Big Easy, The City That Care Forgot, NOLA, The City of Yes, Hollywood South, The Creole City
  • Climate: Humid subtropical, with hot summers, mild winters, and frequent rain
  • Landmarks: French Quarter, Jackson Square, St. Louis Cathedral, Mississippi River, Garden District
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY)
    • Transit: New Orleans Regional Transit Authority (RTA)
    • Highways: I‑610, US 61, US 51, I‑10, US 90
  • Cultural Highlights: Live music, festivals, Creole and Cajun traditions, Mardi Gras, second lines
  • Known For: Mardi Gras
