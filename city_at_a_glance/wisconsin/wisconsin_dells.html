Wisconsin Dells at a Glance

  • State: Wisconsin
  • County: Sauk
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1857
  • Population: ~2,942 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 8.2 mi²
  • Population Density: 359.66 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Dells, The Waterpark Capital of the World
  • Climate: Humid continental, cold winters, warm summers, snowy winters
  • Landmarks: Wisconsin River Dells, Noah’s Ark Waterpark, Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park, Tommy Bartlett Exploratory, Devil’s Lake State Park
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Central Wisconsin Airport (CWA)
    • Transit: NA
    • Highways: WI‑13, US 12
  • Cultural Highlights: Seasonal festivals, live entertainment shows, boat tours, art and craft shops, local history museums
  • Known For: Waterparks
