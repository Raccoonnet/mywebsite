Hot Springs at a Glance

  • State: Arkansas
  • County: Garland
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1851
  • Population: ~37,930 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 37.6 mi²
  • Population Density: 1009.58 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Valley of the Vapors, Spa City
  • Climate: Humid subtropical — hot, humid summers and mild winters; great shoulder seasons
  • Landmarks: Bathhouse Row, Hot Springs National Park, Oaklawn Racing Casino Resort, Gangster Museum of America
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Memorial Field Airport (HOT)
    • Transit: Hot Springs Intracity Transit (HSIT)
    • Highways: US 270, AR‑7, US 70
  • Cultural Highlights: Hot Springs Documentary Film Festival, Valley of the Vapors Indie Music Festival, local galleries, historic spas, and live music venues
  • Known For: Hot Springs National Park
