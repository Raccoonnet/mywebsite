Portland at a Glance

  • State: Oregon
  • County: Multnomah, Washington, Clackamas
  • Time Zone: Pacific Time (PT)
  • Founded: 1845
  • Population: ~652,503 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 145.0 mi²
  • Population Density: 4,500.02 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): City of Roses, Stumptown, Rip City, Beervana, PDX
  • Climate: Temperate marine climate, with mild, wet winters and generally warm, drier summers.
  • Landmarks: Technology, health care, manufacturing, transportation, professional services, creative industries, food and beverage, and tourism.
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Portland International Airport (PDX)
    • Transit: TriMet
    • Highways: I-5, I-205, US-26, OR-99E, OR-213, OR-43, I-84
  • Cultural Highlights: Food carts, independent bookstores, coffee, craft brewing, visual arts, live music, theater, gardens, makers, neighborhood festivals, and diverse cultural communities.
  • Known For: Coffee
