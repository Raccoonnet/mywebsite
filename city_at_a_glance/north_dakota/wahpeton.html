Wahpeton at a Glance

  • State: North Dakota
  • County: Richland
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1887
  • Population: ~8,007 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 5.4 mi²
  • Population Density: 1,495.24 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Home of the Wahpper
  • Climate: Humid continental, with cold, snowy winters and warm summers
  • Landmarks: Chahinkapa Zoo, Richland County Historical Society Museum, North Dakota State College of Science, and the Red River
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Hector International Airport (FAR)
    • Transit: NA
    • Highways: ND-13, ND-127, ND-210
  • Cultural Highlights: Agricultural heritage, college life, community festivals, local history, outdoor recreation, and cross-border connections with Minnesota
  • Known For: Chahinkapa
