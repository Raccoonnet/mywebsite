Chattanooga at a Glance

  • State: Tennessee
  • County: Hamilton
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1839
  • Population: ~181,099 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 150.1 mi²
  • Population Density: 1,206.68 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Scenic City, Chatt, Chattown, Gig City, Nooga, River City
  • Climate: Humid subtropical with warm summers and mild winters
  • Landmarks: Lookout Mountain, Tennessee Riverwalk, Walnut Street Bridge, Tennessee Aquarium
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Chattanooga Metropolitan Airport (CHA)
    • Transit: Chattanooga Area Regional Transportation Authority (CARTA)
    • Highways: I‑75, US 27, I‑24, US 64
  • Cultural Highlights: Outdoor lifestyle, revitalized riverfront, growing arts and food scenes
  • Known For: Scenic outdoors
