Memphis at a Glance

  • State: Tennessee
  • County: Shelby
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1893
  • Population: ~633,104 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 302.6 mi²
  • Population Density: 2,092.42 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Bluff City, Home of the Blues, Grind City, The 901
  • Climate: Humid subtropical with hot summers and mild winters
  • Landmarks: Beale Street, Graceland, National Civil Rights Museum, Mississippi Riverfront
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Memphis International Airport (MEM)
    • Transit: Memphis Area Transit Authority (MATA)
    • Highways: I‑55, US 51, I‑40, I‑240, US 64
  • Cultural Highlights: Blues, soul, rock ’n’ roll, civil rights history, river culture
  • Known For: Blues music
