Denver at a Glance

  • State: Colorado
  • County: Denver
  • Time Zone: Mountain Time (MT)
  • Founded: 1858
  • Population: ~729,019 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 154.7 mi²
  • Population Density: 4,711.68 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Mile High City
  • Climate: Semi-arid — sunny, low humidity, with snowy winters and mild to hot summers
  • Landmarks: Red Rocks Amphitheatre, Union Station, Coors Field, Denver Art Museum, State Capitol
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Denver International Airport (DEN)
    • Transit: Regional Transportation District (RTD)
    • Highways: I‑25, I‑70, US 6, I‑76
  • Cultural Highlights: First Friday art walks, Denver Film Festival, Colorado Symphony, Meow Wolf Denver, thriving live music scene
  • Known For: Mountains
