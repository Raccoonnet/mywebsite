Des Moines at a Glance

  • State: Iowa
  • County: Polk
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1851
  • Population: ~214,133 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 90.7 mi²
  • Population Density: 2360.89 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Niceville USA, Hartford of the West
  • Climate: Humid continental with warm, humid summers, colorful autumns, cold winters, and mild springs
  • Landmarks: Iowa State Capitol, Pappajohn Sculpture Park, Greater Des Moines Botanical Garden, Gray's Lake Park, East Village, Principal Park, Salisbury House & Gardens
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Des Moines International Airport (DSM)
    • Transit: Des Moines Area Regional Transit Authority (DART)
    • Highways: I-235, US-69, IA-163, IA-5, US-6
  • Cultural Highlights: Downtown Farmers' Market, Iowa State Fair, public art, live music, theater, museums, and neighborhood festivals
  • Known For: State Capital
