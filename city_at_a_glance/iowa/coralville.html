Coralville at a Glance

  • State: Iowa
  • County: Johnson
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1873
  • Population: ~22,318 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 12.7 mi²
  • Population Density: 1761.48 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): CoralVegas
  • Climate: Humid continental with warm summers, colorful autumns, cold winters, and pleasant springs
  • Landmarks: Iowa River Landing, Coralville Lake, Devonian Fossil Gorge, Xtream Arena & GreenState Family Fieldhouse, Iowa River Trail
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Eastern Iowa Airport (CID)
    • Transit: Coralville Transit
    • Highways: I-80, US-6
  • Cultural Highlights: Community festivals, performing arts, local markets, university influence, public art, and outdoor recreation
