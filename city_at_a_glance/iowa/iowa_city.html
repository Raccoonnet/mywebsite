Iowa City at a Glance

  • State: Iowa
  • County: Johnson
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1853
  • Population: ~74,828 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 26.2 mi²
  • Population Density: 2857.12 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Athens of Iowa
  • Climate: Humid continental with warm summers, colorful autumns, cold winters, and refreshing springs
  • Landmarks: University of Iowa, Old Capitol Museum, Pedestrian Mall, Kinnick Stadium, Iowa River, Hancher Auditorium, Stanley Museum of Art
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Eastern Iowa Airport (CID)
    • Transit: Iowa City Transit
    • Highways: IA-6
  • Cultural Highlights: UNESCO City of Literature, literary festivals, performing arts, museums, live music, public art, and collegiate traditions
  • Known For: University of Iowa
