Amarillo at a Glance

  • State: Texas
  • County: Potter
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1887
  • Population: ~200,393 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 103.9 mi²
  • Population Density: 1,929.45 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Bomb City, Rotor City, Yellow City
  • Climate: Semi-arid, with hot summers, cold winters, low humidity, frequent wind, and rapid weather changes
  • Landmarks: Cadillac Ranch, the American Quarter Horse Hall of Fame & Museum, Route 66 Historic District, Amarillo Botanical Gardens, and nearby Palo Duro Canyon State Park
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Rick Husband Amarillo International Airport (AMA)
    • Transit: Amarillo City Transit (ACT)
    • Highways: I-27, TX-335, I-40
  • Cultural Highlights: Route 66 heritage, Western and ranching traditions, public art, live music, museums, rodeo culture, and distinctive Texas Panhandle food
  • Known For: Cattle ranching
