Abilene at a Glance

  • State: Texas
  • County: Taylor
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1881
  • Population: ~125,182 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 112.1 mi²
  • Population Density: 1,116.80 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Key City, The Friendly Frontier
  • Climate: Semi-arid, hot summers, mild winters, occasional storms
  • Landmarks: Frontier Texas!, Abilene State Park, Paramount Theatre, National Center for Children’s Illustrated Literature
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Abilene Regional Airport (ABI)
    • Transit: CityLink
    • Highways: US 83, I-20, US 80, US 180
  • Cultural Highlights: Vibrant arts scene, live music, historical museums, annual festivals such as West Texas Fair & Rodeo
  • Known For: Air Force
