Fort Worth at a Glance

  • State: Texas
  • County: Tarrant
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1874
  • Population: ~918,915 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 355.6 mi²
  • Population Density: 2,584.42 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Cowtown, Panther City, Funkytown, Queen City of the Prairie
  • Climate: Humid subtropical, hot summers, mild winters, occasional severe storms
  • Landmarks: Fort Worth Stockyards, Kimbell Art Museum, Amon Carter Museum, Sundance Square, Fort Worth Zoo
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (DFW)
    • Transit: Trinity Metro
    • Highways: I‑35W, US 287, I‑30, I‑20
  • Cultural Highlights: Western heritage, world-class museums, live music venues, rodeos, contemporary art spaces
  • Known For: Cowboys
