Weatherford at a Glance

  • State: Texas
  • County: Parker
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1858
  • Population: ~30,854 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 28.9 mi²
  • Population Density: 1,066.14 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Cutting Horse Captial of the World, Peach Capital of Texas
  • Climate: Humid subtropical with hot summers, mild winters, colorful springs, and pleasant autumns
  • Landmarks: Parker County Courthouse, Historic Downtown Weatherford, Chandor Gardens, Clark Gardens Botanical Park (nearby), Holland Lake Park, Doss Heritage and Culture Center
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (DFW)
    • Transit: NA
    • Highways: TX-171, I-20, US-180, TX-312
  • Cultural Highlights: Peach Festival, historic preservation, western heritage, rodeos, farmers markets, live music, and community events
  • Known For: Peaches
