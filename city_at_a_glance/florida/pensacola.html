Pensacola at a Glance

  • State: Florida
  • County: Escambia
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1822
  • Population: ~54,312 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 41.1 mi²
  • Population Density: 1,320.59 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Upside of Florida, The City of Five Flags, Festival City of the South, Cradle of Naval Aviation
  • Climate: Humid subtropical, with hot summers and mild winters
  • Landmarks: Pensacola Beach, Fort Pickens, Historic Pensacola Village
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Pensacola International Airport (PNS)
    • Transit: Escambia County Area Transit (ECAT)
    • Highways: US 29, FL‑289, US 98, US 90
  • Cultural Highlights: Naval Aviation heritage, historic districts, coastal festivals
  • Known For: Naval aviation
