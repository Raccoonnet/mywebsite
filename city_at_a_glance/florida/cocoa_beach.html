Cocoa Beach at a Glance

  • State: Florida
  • County: Brevard
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1957
  • Population: ~11,354 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 15.2 mi²
  • Population Density: 747.47 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Beach City
  • Climate: Subtropical, with warm temperatures year-round and cooling ocean breezes
  • Landmarks: Cocoa Beach Pier, Lori Wilson Park, Ron Jon Surf Shop
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Orlando International Airport (MCO)
    • Transit: Space Coast Area Transit (SCAT)
    • Highways: US 1, FL‑A1A
  • Cultural Highlights: Surf culture, space-launch viewing, coastal festivals
  • Known For: Surfing
