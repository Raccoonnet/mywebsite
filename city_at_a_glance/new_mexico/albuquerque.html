Albuquerque at a Glance

  • State: New Mexico
  • County: Bernalillo
  • Time Zone: Mountain Time (MT)
  • Founded: 1706
  • Population: ~564,559 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 189.0 mi²
  • Population Density: 2,987.88 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Duke City, ABQ, The 505, Burque, The Q
  • Climate: High-desert climate with abundant sunshine, relatively low precipitation, cool nights, and four distinct seasons.
  • Landmarks: Government, education, health care, technology, scientific research, tourism, aerospace, and service industries.
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Albuquerque Internatonal Sunport (ABQ)
    • Transit: ABQ RIDE
    • Highways: I-29, NM-45, NM-47, NM-423, I-40, NM-528, NM-500, NM-314, NM-556
  • Cultural Highlights: Pueblo and Native American heritage, Hispanic and Latino traditions, New Mexican cuisine, Route 66 history, visual arts, performing arts, museums, festivals, and the Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta.
  • Known For: Hot air balloons
