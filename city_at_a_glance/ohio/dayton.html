Dayton at a Glance

  • State: Ohio
  • County: Montgomery
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1841
  • Population: ~137,644 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 57.0 mi²
  • Population Density: 2,416.50 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Gem City, Birthplace of Aviation
  • Climate: Humid continental—warm summers, crisp autumns, cold winters, and colorful springs
  • Landmarks: National Museum of the United States Air Force, Carillon Historical Park, Dayton Art Institute
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: James M. Cox Dayton International Airport (DAY)
    • Transit: Greater Dayton Regional Transit Authority (GDRTA)
    • Highways: I-70, US-35, I-75
  • Cultural Highlights: A strong performing arts scene, historic neighborhoods like the Oregon District, and year-round festivals
  • Known For: Aviation heritage
