Cleveland at a Glance

  • State: Ohio
  • County: Cuyahoga
  • Time Zone: Eastern Time (ET)
  • Founded: 1796
  • Population: ~365,379 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 82.5 mi²
  • Population Density: 4,429.91 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Forest City
  • Climate: Humid continental with cold, snowy winters and warm, lake-influenced summers
  • Landmarks: Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, West Side Market, Cleveland Museum of Art, Edgewater Park
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Cleveland Hopkins International Airport (CLE)
    • Transit: Greater Cleveland Regional Transit Authority (GCRTA)
    • Highways: I‑77, US 21, US 42, I‑90, I‑480, US 422
  • Cultural Highlights: Major league sports, world-class orchestra, historic neighborhoods, lakefront recreation
  • Known For: Rock Hall
