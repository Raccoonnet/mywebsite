Tulsa at a Glance

  • State: Oklahoma
  • County: Tulsa
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1830
  • Population: ~413,066 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 201.9 mi²
  • Population Density: 2,046.40 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): Oil Capital of the World, Tulsey Town, T-Town, Green Country, Buckle of the Bible Belt, The 918, The Town
  • Climate: Humid subtropical with hot summers, mild winters, and frequent thunderstorms
  • Landmarks: Philbrook Museum of Art, Tulsa Arts District, Gathering Place, Boston Avenue Methodist Church
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Tulsa International Airport (TUL)
    • Transit: MetroLink Tulsa
    • Highways: I‑44, US 75, US 169, US 64, US 412, I‑244
  • Cultural Highlights: Art Deco architecture, music and festival culture, Native American heritage, riverfront recreation
  • Known For: Oil industry
