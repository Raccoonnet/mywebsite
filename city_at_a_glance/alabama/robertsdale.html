Robertsdale at a Glance

  • State: Alabama
  • County: Baldwin
  • Time Zone: Central Time (CT)
  • Founded: 1921
  • Population: ~6,708 (2024 estimate)
  • Area: 6.9 mi²
  • Population Density: 967.97 people/mi²
  • Nickname(s): The Hub of Baldwin County
  • Climate: Humid subtropical, with hot summers, mild winters, and substantial rainfall
  • Landmarks: Baldwin County Fairgrounds, downtown Robertsdale, nearby farms, parks, and access to the broader Gulf Coast
  • Transportation:
    • Nearest Airport: Pensacola International Airport (PNS)
    • Transit: Baldwin Regional Area Transit System (BRATS)
    • Highways: AL-59, US-90, AL-104
  • Cultural Highlights: Baldwin County's agricultural traditions, community events, local churches, Southern foodways, and proximity to the cultural life of the Gulf Coast
  • Known For: Agriculture
